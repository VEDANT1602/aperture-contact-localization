# Rice manuscript — claim-level source checks

Access checked 21 November 2026. The statements below are the manuscript claims each reference supports. The three insertion references have different roles; the fitted curves and counts in the new study are original calculations.

| Ref. | Manuscript statement | Source, location checked and limit |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Interaction and compliance can localize a hole and refine insertion in a physical system. | [Chen et al., full RSS XXI paper](https://www.roboticsproceedings.org/rss21/p060.pdf), Fig. 7a–c and accompanying text on the NIST Assembly Task Board. The figure separates tactile sensing, interaction to reduce hole-location uncertainty and final insertion. Used for prior art only; no transfer of its success rates or device settings. |
| 2 | Active extrinsic contact sensing can guide a peg-in-hole policy. | [Kim & Rodriguez, full author paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.03555), equations (1)–(2) estimate object pose and a contact line from proprioception and tactile displacement. The contact estimate is used in the active insertion pipeline. [MIT record](https://dspace.mit.edu/entities/publication/27bcd744-0801-451f-9f23-f3702abd0902) gives the published 2022 ICRA DOI. Their real contact-sensing setup differs from the synthetic rim scanner. |
| 3 | Mechanical mating involves geometric and force equilibrium. | [Whitney, full original](https://citeseerx.ist.psu.edu/document?doi=f4f7faf5480dd8645146eba58330439862ced921&repid=rep1&type=pdf) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1115/1.3149634), figures 10–11 and the adjacent one- and two-point equilibrium equations. The boundaries separate static, sliding and jamming conditions under the model. It does not calibrate the supplement’s force proxy. |
| 4 | A free conic has five independent parameters and five generic points determine it up to scale. | [Hartley & Zisserman, 2nd ed.](https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9780511811685), §2.2.3, p. 31, Eq. (2.4), “Five points define a conic.” The four-point family and both centers in this manuscript follow by direct substitution. |
| 5 | An ellipse-specific algebraic fit can minimize residuals under the constraint 4AC − B² = 1. | [Fitzgibbon, Pilu & Fisher, article and author materials](https://homepages.inf.ed.ac.uk/rbf/CVonline/LOCAL_COPIES/FITZGIBBON/ELLIPSE/), IEEE TPAMI 21(5), 476–480; direct least-squares objective and ellipticity condition. Our independent implementation eliminates the three linear coefficients before solving the quadratic block; it does not use their source code. |
| 6 | The reference contours belong to projected circles on real camera-calibration patterns. | [Pătrăucean et al., project README](https://github.com/viorik/ELSDc), “Datasets”, entry Dataset2_CalibrationPatterns. The authors credit Higuchi et al.’s imagery and manual labeling of projected circular/ring contours. [Article](https://doi.org/10.1109/TPAMI.2016.2558150). Centers are image annotations, not independently surveyed mechanical centers. |
| 7 | Twenty real ring JPEGs were selected from the 40-image calibration archive. | [Pinned JPEG and annotation mirror](https://github.com/zikai1/Awesome-Ellipse-Detection/tree/4003bef55e929db0797a4586cf352a21c64e3ab1), README “Five real-world datasets,” Calibration.zip archive entries and archive Readme.txt (angle in radians). Downloaded SHA-256 a41f0566347036aec88cd3e58e3fcd029850bdfc912038654259bf3af2b25c7f. The code selects every ring* JPEG. No images are redistributed here. |
| 8 | Real probe alignment, radius compensation and filtering can bias a rim coordinate. | [Stone, Muralikrishnan & Sahay, NIST](https://doi.org/10.6028/jres.116.006), abstract, probe-tip compensation discussion and Fig. 8. Their small-hole metrology identifies failure mechanisms; its micrometre-scale errors are not used to set this peg benchmark’s noise. |

The verified baseline and coefficient/endpoint checks are in the [pinned study](https://github.com/VEDANT1602/aperture-contact-localization/tree/71f76e74192e47e6b9f4c19350aa7b4fb924cb4a). Tables 1–2 trace to the primary and independent simulation outputs. Table 3 traces to contact_budgets.csv; optical medians are computed across per-frame medians from photo_results.csv.
