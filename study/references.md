# Source and reviewer checks

1. Hartley and Zisserman, [publisher](https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9780511811685), [author book site](https://www.robots.ox.ac.uk/~vgg/hzbook/). Second edition §2.2.3, p. 31, equation (2.4), gives five independent homogeneous point constraints on six conic coefficients. This determines the conic up to scale under general position; noisy points need not lie on an ellipse.
2. Fitzgibbon, Pilu and Fisher, [full author copy](https://homepages.inf.ed.ac.uk/rbf/CVonline/LOCAL_COPIES/FITZGIBBON/ELLIPSE/ellipse-pami.pdf), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/34.765658). Section II, pp. 476–477, states the algebraic objective under the ellipticity normalization 4ac−b²=1. The paper's algorithm is the comparison method, not a new estimator here.
3. Stone, Muralikrishnan and Sahay, [full NIST paper](https://doi.org/10.6028/jres.116.006), introduction and Fig. 8. Probe alignment, filtering and finite-tip compensation alter contact-based geometry; the paper supplies no measurement of the per-contact duration for this scanner.
4. NIST, [Peg-in-Hole Data](https://www.nist.gov/el/intelligent-systems-division-73500/peg-hole-data), experimental steps following Table 2 and file definitions under Data, [record DOI](https://doi.org/10.18434/T4/1500929). Registered target poses and insertion outcome are reported. These do not measure individual rim-probe events, center error for unknown ellipses, or this deadline.
   
The superseded optical comparison used a shape prior inferred from the same labels and cannot validate tactile probing. The former 39/35/47 counts referred to a different scoring event and estimator. The new panel discloses all matched fixtures and does not combine those counts with the present endpoints. This geometry note still has no physical probe-time or center-error validation.
